01

The First 3 Minutes: Setting the Tone on Arrival

In residential communities across Benin City—from Government Reserved Area (GRA) to Ugbor and Upper Mission—the first three minutes of your interaction set client confidence for the rest of the job. Clients are not just hiring your pipe cutters or multi-meters; they are welcoming you into their private family environment.

badge 1. Present Digital Credentials

Before entering the doorway or compound, pull up your SabiWork profile card on your phone. Introduce yourself clearly: "Good morning ma, I am Osaze from SabiWork assigned to inspect your booster pump leak."

meeting_room 2. Ask Before Stepping In

Never march straight into living rooms or private hallways unguided. Stand slightly back from the threshold and politely ask: "Please where is the service valve or inspection area located?"

lightbulb
Benin City Field Tip (Compound Etiquette)

Never park your motorcycle or service wagon blocking the client’s main driveway gate in residential compounds along Boundary Road, Ihama, or Limit Road. Always park roadside until instructed, and explicitly verify where the homeowner prefers heavy toolboxes or PVC pipes unloaded.

02

Translating Technical Jargon to Plain Terms

When artisans use high-level workshop jargon, clients often suspect they are being overcharged or manipulated. Speaking plainly builds instant trust and helps homeowners approve your scope without anxiety.

cancel Avoid Industry Jargon

"Madam, your PPR female adaptor suffered severe thermal expansion water hammer and burst the threading."

Client reaction: Confused, anxious, feels out of control.
check_circle Say This Plainly

"The cold-water pipe joint has a hairline crack because booster pump pressure was too heavy. I will install a heavy-duty brass fitting so it never leaks again."

Client reaction: Understands the exact cause and value of the fix.
cancel Avoid Vague Explanations

"The whole plumbing inside your wall is condemned. Everything must change now now."

Client reaction: Alarmed, suspects extortion, stops work.
check_circle Say This Plainly

"Only this 50cm stretch behind the shower mixer is leaking. The rest of the copper riser is fine, so we only need to rebuild this one section today."

Client reaction: Reassured that artisan is honest and precise.
route The 3-Step Plain English Explanation Flow
STAGE 1 Show the Wet Joint

Shine your work light directly on the dripping pipe. Invite them to visually see the water seep.

STAGE 2 State Root Cause

Explain in 10 words or less what worn seal or pressure surge caused the leak to manifest.

STAGE 3 Confirm Quote Scope

Reiterate that this exact replacement is already covered in their S18 platform quote.

03

Handling Unforeseen Complications Without Drama

Plumbing jobs in older compounds often reveal hidden corrosion once tiles or panels are removed. The biggest mistake an artisan can make is demanding sudden cash payments or arguing on-site.

photo_camera
Step-by-Step Escalation Protocol

1. Stop disassembly immediately. Do not break additional wall masonry.
2. Take a clear photo using the SabiWork artisan app camera.
3. Show the homeowner calmly: "Sir, as we uncovered the valve, we found this cast elbow has rusted through. Here is the picture."
4. Create a transparent Add-On Quote via SabiWork so escrow protections cover the extra materials and labor.
