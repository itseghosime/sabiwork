01

The First 3 Minutes: Compound & Doorway Etiquette

In Benin City residential areas—whether visiting GRA, Airport Road, or Ugbowo—first impressions govern client trust immediately. Never assume compound access gives automatic entry to living quarters.

badge Announce Your Arrival

Confirm your name and trade as stated on your SabiWork profile. Give the homeowner 10 seconds to acknowledge before setting heavy toolboxes down.

cleaning_services Drop Cloth & Footwear

Always request permission before entering tiled parlors. Keep clean slip-on protective shoe booties ready in your SabiWork utility bag.

02

Explaining Technical Faults in Everyday Terms

Clients feel anxious when artisans speak in heavy jargon. Rephrase mechanical faults into clear, everyday comparisons without sounding condescending.

Scenario: Leaking PVC Joint Behind Wall Tile
cancel Avoid saying:

“The solvent cement shear pressure failed on the 1-inch elbow joint, madam must break the whole tiles.”

check_circle Recommended phrase:

“The glue seal connecting the cold water pipe has loosened over time. To fix the leak permanently, I will carefully expose two tiles directly behind the tap.”

Scenario: Burned Submersible Pump Relay
cancel Avoid saying:

“Your control box is totally blown, NEPA power surge killed the winding.”

check_circle Recommended phrase:

“A voltage spike tripped the internal switch box to protect your main borehole motor. We can replace just the starter unit rather than the entire pump.”

03

Managing Surprises: In-App Quotes vs Cash

If a client requests extra work outside the booked scope (e.g. asking to service a second bathroom tap while fixing the kitchen sink), handle it through the application directly to preserve booking insurance and payout guarantees.

tips_and_updates
Benin Hub Advisory: Handling Cash Offers

Clients frequently offer cash for unplanned repairs. Politely inform them: “I will submit an immediate add-on scope on SabiWork so our work warranty covers both fixtures.” Cash jobs void dispute mediation and performance badges.
