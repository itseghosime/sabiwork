Executive Summary & Brand Identity SabiWork connects homeowners and local businesses in Benin City with vetted tradespeople (plumbing, electrical, tailoring, carpentry, painting, masonry). Strict Button & Touch Target Specifications 1. **Touch Target Dimensions:** Minimum `height: 44px` across all devices. 2. **Width & Wrapping:** - Content-aware width with `flex-shrink: 0`. - `white-space: nowrap` and `word-break: normal`. - Labels must **never** be clipped, truncated with ellipses mid-word, or squeezed into two broken lines. 3. **Internal Padding:** `horizontal: 20px`, `vertical: 10px` (or `12px` for large CTAs). 4. **Icons:** Fixed-size (`16px` – `20px`) with `flex-shrink: 0` and exact `8px` gap from text. 5. **Narrow Screens & Stacking:** When horizontal space is tight (mobile < 390px, narrow columns), buttons stack vertically or expand to `width: 100%`. They **never** compress side-by-side into illegible states. 6. **Loading State Stability:** Loading buttons preserve their exact pixel dimensions via an internal spinner replacement; they never collapse or expand when triggered. --- ## 6. Master Brand Asset Specification - **Uploaded Asset Structure:** The uploaded brand asset is an **ICON ONLY** (standalone graphic mark). - **Brand Name Text:** The word **SabiWork** must always be rendered as **separate, editable HTML/vector typography** immediately adjacent to the icon. - `Sabi` rendered in Deep Navy (`#0E294B`) - `Work` rendered in Sky Blue (`#38A8E8`) - **Prohibited:** Never bake or flatten the brand name text into the image asset. - **Clearspace:** Minimum `12px` clearspace on all sides. --- ## 7. Conflict Resolutions Documented 1. **Button CTA Color vs Brand Accent:** - *Conflict:* The brand primary accent is light sky-blue (`#38A8E8`), which achieves inadequate contrast (~2.4:1) with white text. - *Resolution:* Primary action buttons use accessible deep sky-blue (`#1478B5`), providing WCAG AA compliant contrast (> 4.5:1) against `#FFFFFF`. Light sky-blue (`#38A8E8`) is used for highlights, badges, borders, and brand accents. 2. **Escrow Terminology vs Platform Compliance:** - *Conflict:* Early specifications referenced government certifications, NIN/BVN APIs, and production bank escrow. - *Resolution:* All financial flows are labeled strictly as **"Paystack TEST Mode"** demo milestone transactions, and verification is framed as **"Admin-Reviewed Profile"**. Fictional sample data in Benin City is explicitly maintained. 3. **Green Color Hierarchy:** - *Conflict:* Older design system versions used green as the core theme. - *Resolution:* Green is strictly relegated to semantic success indicators (`#10B981`, `#ECFDF5`). Blue (`#38A8E8` / `#1478B5` / `#0E294B`) is the exclusive brand foundation.
