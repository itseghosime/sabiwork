# Discovery assets

- `sabiwork-icon.png`: the project leader’s previously approved sky-blue icon-only mark. It is displayed fully with separate editable brand text.
- `artisan-demo-sheet.png`: generated on 29 October 2026 for this student prototype, showing five illustrative Nigerian artisans. These are fictional people, not actual providers or endorsements. Missing photos use initials.

Images are local. Locally bundled `@fontsource` packages provide the two fonts; their licence files remain in those packages. Fonts are loaded through `next/font/local`.
